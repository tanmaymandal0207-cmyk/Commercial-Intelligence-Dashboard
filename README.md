# Commercial Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview
An end-to-end Power BI dashboard built to analyze commercial performance, product intelligence, pricing behavior, and lifecycle risk across a multi-year automotive product portfolio.

---

## Business Objective
The objective of this project was to identify:

- Revenue concentration patterns
- High-performing and declining products
- Pricing efficiency
- Product lifecycle risks
- Seasonal performance trends

---

## Dashboard Modules

### Executive Overview
- Revenue trends
- Product segmentation
- Quarterly performance
- YoY KPI tracking

### Product Intelligence
- Product positioning matrix
- Pareto analysis
- Revenue share analysis
- Product concentration mapping

### Pricing Intelligence
- ASP trend analysis
- Premium product tracking
- Revenue per unit analysis
- Pricing distribution

### Lifecycle & Risk
- Growth vs declining products
- Volatility analysis
- Seasonal product performance
- Revenue concentration risk

- Premium products show higher revenue efficiency
- High volatility products contribute major commercial risk
